About Branch Management

Branches represent physical office locations or regional centers within your organization. Each branch can contain multiple departments and users.

  • Purpose: Organize your company by geographical locations
  • Structure: Company → Branch → Department → Section
  • Benefits: Location-based reporting, escalation routing, and access control
