About Employee Types

Employee Types are used to classify employees based on their employment status or contract type.

  • Purpose: Categorize employees (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Intern)
  • Usage: Used in HR reporting, access control, and workflow routing.
  • Benefits: Better employee segmentation and targeted policies
